Project Title: Speech Enhancement versus Speech Recognition: Evaluating STFT Wiener Filtering Under Noise
GitHub Pages: https://xinyu-shen-334.github.io/elec5305-project-530537427/
GitHub Repository: https://github.com/xinyu-shen-334/elec5305-project-530537427

Following my teacher's feedback, I have revised the research focus to investigate whether STFT-based Wiener filtering improves speech intelligibility and automatic speech recognition, rather than only increasing waveform SNR. I have developed an initial Python implementation featuring controlled noise mixing, STFT analysis, Wiener gain estimation, inverse STFT, and SNR, waveform and spectrogram comparisons. The prototype also compares a practical noise-spectrum estimate from an independent calibration segment with an oracle estimate based on the known added noise. I generated preliminary plots using synthetic signals to check the processing pipeline; these are not real-speech or ASR results. Next, I plan to evaluate transcribed LibriSpeech audio mixed with DEMAND environmental noises at different input SNRs, using STOI and Word Error Rate from a fixed pretrained Whisper recogniser. I will examine whether SNR improvements correspond to lower WER and how estimation accuracy changes performance.
